# The Transnational Ayahuasca Empire: How Alberto José Varela and Inner Mastery Industrialized Spiritual Extractivism
**By Clement Vane**
*Investigative Reference: NMSC-DOSSIER-LATAM-003*

The ethereal mist clinging to the Amazonian canopy, the purported scent of ancient wisdom, the promise of profound healing – these are the lures employed by a new breed of predatory enterprise. At the vanguard of this extractive industry stood the late **Alberto José Varela** and his sprawling corporate apparatus, **Inner Mastery International S.L.** (operating through front brands such as *Ayahuasca International*, *Conscious School*, and *Beyond Inner*). This multinational network systematically stripped ancestral entheogens of their cultural context, community safeguards, and sacred reciprocity, transforming them into a high-priced, mass-produced commodity marketed across Europe and Latin America.

## The Corporate Machine: Inner Mastery International S.L.

Inner Mastery International S.L., incorporated in Spain, served as the operational command for a franchise network spanning Spain, Italy, Germany, the Netherlands, Mexico (Tulum), and Colombia (Minca and the Putumayo river basin). Varela pioneered a clinical, corporate sales funnel:
* **Hyper-Commodified Retreats:** Weekend sessions charging between **$1,500 and $5,000 per participant**, completely divorcing ancestral medicines from long-term communal integration.
* **Westernized Jargon:** Blending sacred brews with California biohacking jargon, aggressive neuro-linguistic programming, and New Age corporate coaching.
* **Lineage Tokenism:** Disenfranchised indigenous elders were hired as decorative props for European promotional tours and websites, while over **90% of revenues were funneled directly into Varela's Spanish corporate entities**.

## Formal Indigenous Resistance: UMIYAC and ONIC Communiqués

The cultural usurpation perpetrated by Varela triggered an unprecedented formal revolt from legitimate ancestral authorities:
* **UMIYAC (Unión de Médicos Indígenas Yageceros de la Amazonía Colombiana):** Issued historic, public communiqués declaring Alberto José Varela *persona non grata*. UMIYAC explicitly condemned Varela for the industrial trafficking of Yagé, the commercial trivialization of ancestral rites, and the unauthorized creation of pseudo-shamanic "schools" lacking any lineage mandate.
* **ONIC (Organización Nacional Indígena de Colombia):** Denounced Inner Mastery International for biopiracy, epistemic extractivism, and the commodification of sacred botanical patrimony without Free, Prior, and Informed Consent.

## Criminal Repercussions: Raids, Arrests, and Public Health Violations

The aggressive commercialization of entheogens outside certified medical or traditional frameworks led to severe legal and physical fallout:
1. **European Law Enforcement Crackdowns:** Judicial police forces in Spain, France, and Italy executed multiple raids on Inner Mastery compounds, resulting in arrests and indictments for smuggling controlled psychoactive substances (DMT), crimes against public health, and the illegal practice of medicine (*intrusismo profesional*).
2. **The "European School of Ayahuasca" Pipeline:** Varela instituted rapid, weekend training programs where paying European novices were certified as "facilitators" after minimal exposure, unleashing unqualified operators into high-risk psychological environments.
3. **Severe Human Cost:** Documented cases of chemical incapacitation, acute psychotic decompensation, and predatory sexual abuse perpetrated by facilitators against participants immobilized in altered states.
